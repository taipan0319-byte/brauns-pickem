Engine A · NFL win probability

Engine B · Pool strategy

Objective: Ryan's probability of finishing first, not expected correct picks. Monte Carlo over the remaining schedule with common random numbers: game outcomes drawn from Engine A probabilities, each opponent's pick drawn from their fitted tendency and team lean, standings carried forward, ties split evenly. For every game this week the engine compares P(first) with the favorite against P(first) with the underdog, jointly across the week. Robustness re-runs the comparison with the family modeled half as contrarian and twice as contrarian; LOW means the sign flips or the effect is inside noise, and LOW resolves to the favorite.
